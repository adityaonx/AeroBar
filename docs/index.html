What it adds.

Window tabs and the Start Menu belong to the AeroBar modes. Previews, the switcher, clipboard history and the shelf work in all four.

Every window, one click away

In the taskbar modes, open windows become live tabs. Hover for a live preview with a close button, or press + for a new window. Badge counts carry over from the Dock, and the active tab can show as a pill, line, dot or border.

A Start Menu with real depth

Click the Aero Orb to search apps and files, pin favourites to a drag-to-reorder grid, pick up recent files, and lock, log out, restart or shut down without leaving the menu.

⌘Tab, rebuilt

A Smart Switcher with live window previews. ⌘Tab moves between apps and ⌘` between windows of one app. Include apps with no windows, go minimal, or pause it in the apps that need the system switcher.

Clipboard, shelf, music and stats

Music controls for Spotify, Apple Music or any web player. CPU, RAM and network at a glance. A shelf to park files and drag them out later. Clipboard history with an encrypted, password-protected backup. Each has its own hotkey.

Put it on any edge

Bottom, top, left or right, as a taskbar or a dock. Prefer the real thing? Keep the macOS Dock and add AeroBar’s glass, indicators and extensions around it.

And everything else

Dynamic Tint and AuraBarThe bar samples the active window’s colour, with per-app overrides. AuraBar carries the same glass to the menu bar.
Multiple displaysShow the bar on every display, the main one or only external monitors, with per-display settings and a dimmer for idle screens.
Trackpad gesturesSwipe with three fingers to switch AeroBar windows, with adjustable sensitivity.
Global hotkeysAssign a shortcut to the clipboard, shelf, widgets, Quick Links, Screen Search, Show Desktop, Mission Control and the bin.
Screen Search and Quick LinksSearch what’s on screen with Google Lens, and drag URLs onto the bar to keep them one click away.
Tasks, Mission Control, Show DesktopA dropdown of open windows grouped by app, plus buttons for Mission Control, Show Desktop and the Trash.
Pinned apps and foldersDrag apps into the tray, pin folders to the shelf, and right-click any tab to pin its app.
Glass, tunedStart from Frosted Ice, Obsidian Dark, Vibrant Mint, Satin Crimson, Neon Amethyst or Industrial Amber, then adjust tint, strength, borders and bar height. The Dock gets its own glass panel in Dock mode.
Focus ModeDim non-hovered items and the wallpaper so your attention stays where it belongs. A separate dimmer covers idle displays.
Safer quittingRequire ⌘Q or ⌘W twice to quit an app or close a window, and quit an app automatically when you close its last window.
No telemetryNo analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking. Diagnostics stay on your Mac unless you share them.
OfflineAeroBar never connects to the internet. Links open in your browser only after you say yes. The beta code page keeps a small record per install; the Privacy Policy lists it.
Signed updatesThe Update Basket checks each release’s signature before it installs anything.

Install in one command.

  1. Run the command, then open AeroBar.
  2. If macOS says the app is damaged, run the second command.
  3. Grant Accessibility access when asked.

Install

brew tap adityaonx/aerobar https://github.com/adityaonx/AeroBar && brew trust adityaonx/aerobar && HOMEBREW_NO_QUARANTINE=1 brew install --cask aerobar

If macOS says the app is damaged

xattr -rd com.apple.quarantine /Applications/AeroBar.app

Prefer a disk image? Download the DMG from the latest release. AeroBar isn’t notarized yet, so open it once from System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Homebrew installs AeroBar; later updates come through the Update Basket.
